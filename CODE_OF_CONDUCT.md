# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Octifine to be a friendly and welcoming place for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity or experience. Everyone who takes part in the project, on GitHub or in any Octifine community space, agrees to follow this code of conduct.

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
* Give and accept feedback kindly, and focus on the work, not the person.
* Keep discussions on topic and useful.
* Respect other people's privacy and their right to disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
* Hateful, sexual or violent content, or slurs of any kind.
* Sharing someone's private information, such as their address, real name or account details, without their permission.
* Promoting cheats, exploits, account stealing or scams.
* Spam, advertising, or repeatedly derailing conversations.
* Encouraging anyone else to do any of the above.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every Octifine space, which is the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews). It also applies when someone represents Octifine anywhere else.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, report it privately in whichever way you prefer:

* **GitHub:** open the **...** menu on the comment, issue or pull request and choose **Report content**.

Include what happened, where it happened (links or screenshots help) and when. Reports are kept confidential, and nobody who reports in good faith will face retaliation.

## Enforcement

Octifine maintainers review every report and decide on a response. Depending on how serious it is, that can be:

1. A private warning that explains what was wrong.
2. A temporary ban from the GitHub repository.
3. A permanent ban.

Maintainers may also remove, edit or reject comments, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct.
